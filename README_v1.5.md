# Living Terrarium 3D v1.5

## Changes
- Six world-space stance foot anchors, alternating tripod swing, eased foot landing and toe segments.
- Ground contact raycasts against existing collision shapes for substrate, rock and wood.
- Small additional thorax and abdomen geometric details.
- Foot reach clamping and emergency replant on abrupt displacement.

## Limitations
- Procedural geometry only; no Blender rigged GLB or photoreal asset.
- CLIMB remains an animation preview, not autonomous vertical climbing.
- Raycasts/IK and gameplay require Godot runtime testing; not validated on a device.
- Android APK not built.
